# WEXFORD — Multi-page interactive website

Open `index.html` in a modern browser.

## Pages
- `index.html` — immersive landing page
- `about.html` — Wexford story
- `catalogue.html` — filterable collection (15 catalogue entries)
- `contact.html` — enquiry page
- `products/*.html` — 15 individual product detail pages

## Asset source
Product photographs are extracted directly from the supplied `WEXFORD ITEM DETAILS.pdf`. The low resolution is inherited from the source PDF. Lifestyle interiors are third-party remote images, for design illustration only. Wexford logo comes from earlier supplied logo artwork.

## Interactions
Scroll-reading progress indicator, IntersectionObserver reveal animations, sticky scroll-driven story (three chapters), collection filters with shareable URL, hover zooms, magnetic CTA effects, mobile menu, smooth motion, FAQ disclosure, and prefilled product enquiries. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.

## Important before launch
The PDF describes wardrobe/interior fittings rather than home appliances. Product descriptions are short editorial summaries, not manufacturer-certified performance claims. The PDF does not list prices, inventory, warranties, or verified installation requirements. Product dimension units are assumed millimetres where format suggests them; double-check with Wexford. The contact form only drafts an email without a recipient: configure a secure backend and confirmed business contact address. Replace remote lifestyle imagery with licensed final assets. Test on mobile and desktop before publication.
